## Full description

Billiards where you can't aim.

Every stroke, the cue spins like a roulette wheel and lands wherever it likes. So you bend the
table instead. Drag the corners and the pockets ride along. Add new bends to the rails, drop in
bumpers, walls, portals and magnets. Then pick how hard to smack the ball, and with how much spin.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY

• Free Play: clear all ten balls in as few strokes as you can. Every game deals a remixed table:
diamonds, bowties, L-shaped lounges, with chomping pockets, bouncy rails and toys to place.
• Classic: The Rec Room. Twelve hand-made puzzle tables, each built around a trick. Earn up to
three stars. Lose, and the table gets flipped.
• Toy Box: every toy, no limits. Build ridiculous tables, rewind a shot, respin the cue.

PLAN THE IMPOSSIBLE SHOT

• A preview shows where the cue ball and every ball it hits will go.
• Put English on the cue ball: screw back, follow through, swerve around a bumper.
• Combos, banks and portal trick shots earn style points and fireworks.

TOYS AND TROUBLE

Bumpers, glass panes, one-way gates, speed pads, ice, mud, sand, conveyor belts, magnets, black
holes and portals. Picky pockets, gentle pockets, pockets that open and shut on a beat. Bowling
balls, eggs that crack, bombs that go off, and a chicken that runs away from the cue ball.

SHARE YOUR BEST SHOT

Replay any shot exactly as it happened, then send a link: your friends watch the very same shot
and can play your table.

No ads. No accounts. Plays offline.
