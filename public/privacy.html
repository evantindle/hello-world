Privacy

Bendy Billiards collects no data.

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It never sends anything about you or how you play to anyone.

Your progress (stars, best scores, whether the sound is muted) is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

If you tap SHARE, the game makes a link that describes that one shot (the table, the balls and how you hit them) and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded: the shot lives inside the link itself, and only goes wherever you choose to send it.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/evantindle/hello-world.
